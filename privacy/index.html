Privacy policy

What I collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Last updated 28 October 2026.

Who I am

I'm Oliver Steadman, and I run Ollie Type 1 Travel and Type 1 Travel Club from Eastbourne, East Sussex, in the UK. I'm the person responsible for your personal information. You can contact me at hello@ollietype1travel.com.

What I collect

  • When you join a list or download a resource: your name and email address, and which form you used.
  • If you fill in the travel preferences survey: the countries you're interested in, the months and years you could travel, an approximate budget range, an age range and who you'd travel with. All of it is optional.
  • Email activity: whether you open or click my emails, so I can see what's useful.

I don't ask for medical details and I don't need them. Please don't send me any health information in a form or reply unless you want to.

Why I use it

I use your details to send you the emails you signed up for, to plan trips that people actually want to join, and to tell you about relevant travel updates. I do this with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any email.

Who else handles it

  • Kit is the email service that stores your details and sends my emails. It's based in the United States, so your information may be processed outside the UK.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website, protects it from spam and bots, and runs the check on the survey page.
  • Some pages carry travel booking widgets and affiliate links, such as GetYourGuide and Stay22. These companies have their own privacy policies and may use cookies.

I don't sell your information.

How long I keep it

I keep your details until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete them. Unsubscribing stops the emails. If you want your information removed completely, email me.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information I hold about you, correct it, delete it, or object to how I use it. You can also update your travel preferences at any time: ask for a personal link on the preferences page or reply to any of my emails.

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. I'd appreciate the chance to put it right first.

Changes

If I change this policy, I'll update the date at the top.
