About

Hi, I’m Ollie

I’m a 25-year-old UK-based solo travel creator sharing what travel with Type 1 diabetes and severe allergies actually looks like — the good bits, the stressful bits, and the bits where a fridge gets far too involved.

Background

Travel, psychology and content

I started the Type 1 solo travel platform in January 2026 after building content experience for around two years. I also have a sales background and a Master of Science in Psychology, which helps me understand attention, trust and why people act.

Why this exists

To make travel feel possible, not perfect

I’m not here to pretend travelling with diabetes and allergies is effortless. It takes planning, backup plans and occasional admin-heavy nonsense. But it can be done, and honest information makes it feel less intimidating.
