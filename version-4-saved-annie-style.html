Hi, I'm Ollie

I'm a 26-year-old UK-based solo travel creator producing authentic, performance-led content for hostels, hotels, tours, travel experiences and remote travel brands.

My perspective is shaped by travelling independently with Type 1 diabetes and severe allergies. It gives my content a clear narrative: real planning, real risk management, real decisions and a genuine reason for audiences to pay attention.

For tours, hostels, hotels and experiences, I help answer the questions anxious travellers are already asking: Is this safe for me? Can I store insulin properly? Will staff understand? Can I book this with confidence?

Alongside two years of content creation experience, I have a background in sales and hold a Master of Science in Psychology. That combination gives me a strong understanding of attention, emotion, trust and decision-making — the same principles that make short-form content memorable, persuasive and worth acting on.

I launched my Type 1 solo travel platform in January 2026. Since then, across Instagram, TikTok and Facebook, my content has reached 12M+ views/viewers, generated 350K+ interactions and prompted 300+ messages from people actively asking for advice on travelling safely and confidently.

I create UGC partnerships and hosted collaborations that feel like a trusted traveller sharing a valuable experience, while still being built around the brand's goals.

What sets me apart

I create travel content that feels credible, not scripted.

Many travel ads are overproduced and easy to swipe past. I create content that feels like it came from someone genuinely travelling, evaluating the experience and showing why it is worth booking.

I combine lived experience with psychology.

My MSc in Psychology helps me understand what captures attention, builds trust and moves people from interest to action. Paired with my lived experience, this makes each piece of content more strategic than a simple travel montage.

I reduce booking anxiety for health-conscious travellers.

My content can show how a tour, hostel, hotel or experience supports people who travel with chronic illness, allergies or medical equipment — from safe insulin storage to feeling confident that the experience is manageable.

I know how to turn attention into useful action.

Recent content has reached millions organically, including 6.66M Instagram views in 90 days and 5.8M TikTok viewers across 365 days, with hundreds of people messaging for advice, recommendations and practical travel support.

I understand content that converts.

Alongside my travel platform, I have hands-on social commerce experience through TikTok Shop, generating approximately £8,000 in revenue across a six-month period under a separate content persona. This strengthened my understanding of product-led storytelling, conversion-focused hooks and what makes audiences take action.

I can support both destination and remote campaigns.

I create onsite content for hostels, hotels, tours and experiences, as well as remote UGC for apps, eSIMs, insurance, travel planning tools and safety-focused brands.
