Phnom Penh to Siem Reap

Explore Cambodia with Ollie

Cambodia was the country that made me stay. Three days in Siem Reap became nine. Now I'm going back with the community: temples, islands, history, Khmer food and people who understand why your bag is mostly insulin.

  • Angkor Wat sunrise and Ta Prohm with a local guide
  • Private long-tail boat and snorkelling on Koh Rong Sanloem
  • Kayaking, a pepper plantation and a Khmer cooking class in Kampot
  • Phnom Penh's history, including S21 and the Killing Fields

Day by day

Day 1Arrive in Phnom Penh

Airport pickup, settle into the hotel and meet the group over a welcome dinner.

Day 2Phnom Penh's history

A tuk-tuk tour, then S21 Prison and the Killing Fields to learn about Cambodia's recent past.

Day 3Head to Kampot

Travel to our riverside guesthouse and spend the afternoon kayaking through the countryside.

Day 4Food and a river sunset

A pepper plantation, a Khmer cooking class and a sunset river cruise.

Day 5Koh Rong Sanloem

Train and fast ferry to the island, then beach time and sunset.

Day 6Boat trip and snorkelling

A private long-tail boat to snorkelling spots and quieter beaches.

Day 7Beach morning, then north

A last island morning, the ferry back and an overnight bus to Siem Reap.

Day 8Siem Reap and floating villages

Explore the countryside around Siem Reap and its traditional floating villages.

Day 9Angkor Wat and APOPO

Sunrise at Angkor Wat, Ta Prohm with a local guide, APOPO's landmine-detection rats and a final evening together.

Day 10Onward travel

The trip ends in Siem Reap. Head home or stay on; the group leader can help with plans.

Good to know

Questions before you book

Still unsure? Message me on WhatsApp.

Do I need to have Type 1 to come?

No. The trips are built for people living with Type 1, and partners, friends and family are welcome too.

Who organises the trip?

KILROY arranges the accommodation, transport, guide and listed activities, and handles bookings and payments. I'm there the whole time alongside an experienced local guide.

What's included?

Accommodation, transport between stops, a guide and the listed activities. It isn't all-inclusive: flights and some meals and costs are extra. Check KILROY's page for the full list.

Is there medical support on the trip?

I'm a fellow traveller, not a medical professional. You manage your own diabetes, and I'd always suggest speaking to your diabetes team before you travel.

Can I come on my own?

Yes. Most people do. That's exactly why these trips exist.
