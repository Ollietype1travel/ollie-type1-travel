About Ollie · 11K+ followers across platforms

Five months across Southeast Asia

I had wanted to travel the world since I was about twelve. Then COVID arrived, followed by a Type 1 diabetes diagnosis. I already lived with severe peanut and shellfish allergies.

In early 2026, I booked a one-way flight to Bangkok and travelled through Thailand, Laos, Vietnam, Cambodia, Indonesia and Malaysia. My insulin froze in northern Vietnam, I lost a bank card in Hanoi and I stayed in some dodgy hostels which my mum would probably have a mental breakdown if she saw.

A cause close to me

Supporting Action4Diabetes

I volunteered with Action4Diabetes during my travels in Southeast Asia. The charity supports local people living with Type 1 diabetes with insulin, glucose monitoring and peer support.
