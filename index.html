About me

I booked a one-way flight to Bangkok anyway.

I was diagnosed with Type 1 diabetes at 20 and already lived with severe peanut and shellfish allergies. For a while I thought the solo travel I wanted probably was not sensible anymore.

In 2026 I spent five months backpacking through Southeast Asia and started this site to share what actually worked, what went wrong and what I would do differently.
