What sets me apart

I create travel content that feels credible, not scripted.

Many travel ads are overproduced and easy to swipe past. I create content that feels like it came from someone genuinely travelling, evaluating the experience and showing why it is worth booking.

I combine lived experience with psychology.

My MSc in Psychology helps me understand what captures attention, builds trust and moves people from interest to action. Paired with my lived experience, this makes each piece of content more strategic than a simple travel montage.

I reduce booking anxiety for health-conscious travellers.

My content can show how a tour, hostel, hotel or experience supports people who travel with chronic illness, allergies or medical equipment — from safe insulin storage to feeling confident that the experience is manageable.

I know how to turn attention into useful action.

Recent content has reached millions organically, including 6.66M Instagram views in 90 days and 5.8M TikTok viewers across 365 days, with hundreds of people messaging for advice, recommendations and practical travel support.

I understand content that converts.

Alongside my travel platform, I bring practical experience in social commerce and sales-led content. This has strengthened my understanding of product-led storytelling, conversion-focused hooks and what makes audiences click, enquire, book or buy.

I can support both destination and remote campaigns.

I create onsite content for hostels, hotels, tours and experiences, as well as remote UGC for apps, eSIMs, insurance, travel planning tools and safety-focused brands.

A note on in-kind opportunities

Complimentary stays, experiences and premium access are hugely appreciated, especially when they fit my travel plans. I still treat the work like a campaign: planning hooks, filming, editing and making sure the final content aligns with your goals. For larger briefs, I prioritise partnerships that include payment alongside the in-kind offer.

If you are a charity or purpose-led organisation aligned with Type 1 diabetes, chronic illness, allergies, safe travel or community support, I am open to meeting you and helping spread awareness free of charge where the mission genuinely aligns with my niche and values.
