---
name: Analytics evidence
description: Durable rule for presenting creator analytics sourced from platform screenshots.
---

Platform analytics must retain the reporting window and action type shown by the source. Views, reach, interactions, likes, comments, and shares are not interchangeable, and values from different platform windows must not be presented as one deduplicated total.

**Why:** Platform screenshots can show different date ranges and definitions; summing them without scope creates a misleading partnership claim.

**How to apply:** When updating portfolio or media-kit copy, publish platform-specific cards with explicit windows, and keep older verified snapshots separate from current ones.
