About / positioning

Travel content with stakes built in.

I’m Ollie, a UK travel creator documenting what ambitious solo travel actually looks like when you also have Type 1 diabetes and severe peanut and shellfish allergies.

That means the story might begin with a mountain road, jungle trek or slow boat — and suddenly involve insulin storage, finding a pharmacy, explaining an allergy in another language or solving a problem thousands of miles from home.

I make those realities part of the adventure rather than turning the adventure into a medical lecture.
