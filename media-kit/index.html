Why Ollie

I notice the practical details other travel content can miss.

My lived experience with Type 1 diabetes and severe allergies gives my travel content a practical perspective. I naturally notice the details that can turn uncertainty into confidence.

That includes storage, food information, transport, communication, access and safety. I include what matters without making the content feel clinical or losing the excitement of the experience.

My approach is story-first, useful and human. My background in psychology and sales helps me build strong hooks, clear stories and content that feels natural rather than scripted.
